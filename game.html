🎛️ Music Studio

Tempo bpm

Click or drag on the grid to add and remove notes (the notes are all in key, so it always sounds good) · pick a track above and give it an instrument · Space plays and stops · Done turns the song into a music disc — craft a Jukebox (8 planks + 1 diamond), place it and right-click it with the disc: the song plays all over the world · Esc closes

🎸 Guitar

Click a string to pluck it (further right = higher fret) · Keys Z X C V B N pluck the six open strings · 1–6 strum chords (Em, Am, C, D, G, A) · Space strums again · E acoustic / electric · Esc puts it away

Hold the right mouse button and move the mouse to look around. Left click mines the block under your cursor.
Survival: gravity, fall damage, mining, inventory (36 slots), crafting, day/night cycle with zombies and skeletons at night, caves with iron and diamonds (diamonds need an iron pickaxe).
Creative: no damage, unlimited blocks, fly with Space (up) and Shift (down).
The game mode is chosen before you start a world and can't be changed afterwards — press New world (or N) to pick again.
AI players (survival): they gather wood, mine stone, iron and diamonds, craft tools, swords and armor, and fight monsters.
